Waypoint 01 — Origin

A signal, before it's an event.

Techfest started as a late-night build session between three engineering students who wanted an excuse to stay up arguing about architecture. It's now three days of hackathons, robotics, talks, and games — but the argument is still the point.

Every track is judged on whether it actually works, not how well it pitches. Bring something half-finished. Leave with it running.
